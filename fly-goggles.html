▸ Saving to your phone & posting to Facebook

iPhone. "Save image" puts the picture in Files, not Photos. Open the Files app, go to Downloads, tap the image, hit the share icon, then Save Image. Now it's in your camera roll and Instagram can see it.

Android. It lands in your gallery automatically. Just open Instagram and pick it.

If the button doesn't work — take a screenshot of the graphic and crop it square. Works just as well.

Posting to Facebook? Same graphic, same caption, but trim the hashtags down to two or three. A long hashtag block reads as spam on Facebook in a way it doesn't on Instagram.

Kit 02 · Phase 3. Posts GOG-01 through GOG-07 cover the standard goggle: polycarbonate lens with anti-scratch and anti-fog coatings, polyurethane frame, quick-change lens with pre-installed tear-off posts, three-layer face foam, 40mm adjustable double buckle strap with non-slip silicone, adult and youth sizes, nose guard compatible on adult versions, microfiber bag with adult versions. GOG-08 is the sand goggle and its spec is different — closed cell vent foam and two-layer face foam, and the tear-off posts and bag are not part of that listing. Don't mix the two. Lens colors and accessories vary, so confirm what you stock before pointing customers at a specific tint.
